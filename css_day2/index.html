About Our Farmhouse

Nestled in the heart of lush countryside, Earthy Escape Farmhouse offers a perfect blend of rustic charm and modern comfort. Spread across 15 acres of pristine farmland, our property provides an idyllic retreat from the hustle and bustle of city life.


Established in 2015, our family-run farmhouse has been welcoming guests for over three decades. We take pride in offering authentic farm experiences, fresh organic produce, and warm hospitality that makes every guest feel at home.


Whether you're looking for a peaceful weekend getaway, a family celebration venue, or a unique corporate retreat, Earthy Escape Farmhouse provides the perfect setting with its scenic beauty and tranquil atmosphere.
